What platforms do you work on?
+
I'm available on Fiverr, Upwork, and for direct contracts via WhatsApp or email. All platforms are active and I typically respond within a few hours.

+
Simple automation bots typically take 2–5 days. Complex systems with multiple integrations or n8n workflows can take 1–2 weeks. I always provide a timeline estimate before starting.
Do you offer revisions after delivery?
+
Yes — I include free revisions for all projects. My goal is to make sure the solution works exactly as expected. I also offer ongoing maintenance packages for long-term support.
Can you work with my existing codebase?
+
Absolutely. I can integrate automation solutions into existing Python, Django, Flask, or FastAPI projects. Just share the repo and I'll review it before giving a quote.
Is the automation legal and safe?
+
I build automation for legitimate use cases like data collection, workflow efficiency, and process automation. I always advise clients on the terms of service of the platforms involved.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
Payments are handled through Fiverr or Upwork for security. For direct clients, I accept bank transfer or mobile banking. 50% upfront for larger projects.
Do you build n8n workflows from scratch?
+
Yes — I design and build complete n8n workflows including Facebook automation, YouTube bots, AI pipelines, email scrapers, and any multi-app integration you need.
How do I get started?
+
Simply message me on WhatsApp, send an email, or reach out via Fiverr/Upwork. Describe your project and I'll get back to you within a few hours with a plan and quote.
